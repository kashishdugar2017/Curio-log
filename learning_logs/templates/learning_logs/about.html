About Curio Log

Curio Log is a personal learning journal for curious minds. It gives you a place to collect questions, explore ideas, record discoveries, and keep track of what you learn.

Learning can happen anywhere and can involve almost anything. You might be curious about programming, mathematics, science, history, languages, sports, technology, or a completely new subject.

With Curio Log, you can organize your curiosities into topics and add discoveries to each one. Over time, your Curio Log becomes a personal collection of things you have explored and learned.

The goal is simple: explore what interests you, record what you discover, and keep learning.
